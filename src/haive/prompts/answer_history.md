Answer template (history): a "Timeline" section, oldest first, with one claim per piece of work: group related commits (the same feature, fix, or doc update) into one claim that cites each of them, and skip merges and trivial commits. Start every claim's text with the date as YYYY-MM-DD (from the commits' evidence), or a date range for a group, then what changed and why (from the commit messages). The history-window item says whether the commits listed are all of the window's: if they are, gaps mean no activity then (e.g. "all the work fell in one week"), not missing evidence. Cite each commit with a CommitRef {repo, sha} using the sha from its evidence header. Where current code from the evidence shows the result of a change, you may add a RepoRef to those lines. Finish with a short "Why" section.
